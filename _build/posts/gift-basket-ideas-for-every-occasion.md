A gift basket can feel generic or it can feel like it was made for one person only. The difference is not how much goes inside. It is how much thought goes into what goes inside. When a basket is built around the person, every item says something about them. Here is how to plan one that lands.

## Start with the person

Before you think about items, think about the person. What do they do on a slow Sunday? What do they always run out of? What makes them laugh? Jot down three or four things you know they love. Those notes become the plan for the whole basket.

This also helps you skip the filler. If they do not drink coffee, a mug of fancy beans is just clutter. A few well chosen items beat a pile of random ones every time.

## Pick one theme

A theme ties everything together and makes the basket look intentional. It can be simple. A few that work well:

- **Cozy night in:** a soft throw, a personalized mug, tea or hot chocolate and a good book
- **Kitchen lover:** an engraved board, a favourite sauce, a recipe card in your handwriting
- **Self care:** a robe or towel with their initials, a candle and a few calming treats
- **New home:** a welcome sign or coaster set, a plant and something for the first meal in the new place
- **Big birthday:** a keepsake with the age or year, their favourite snacks and a small photo gift

Once you have a theme, every item should fit it. If something does not, leave it out.

## Make one piece the star

Every great basket has a centrepiece. This is the item they will keep long after the snacks are gone. Personalized pieces make the best stars because nobody else could have given them. Think a tumbler with their name, an engraved journal, a photo crystal of a favourite memory or a ribbon printed with a birthday message.

Build the rest of the basket around that one piece. Smaller items can be fun and useful, but the star is what makes it feel special.

> **Tip** Put the personalized piece where it is seen first when the wrap comes off. It sets the tone for everything else.

## Think about the finishing touches

How a basket looks when it is handed over matters. A sturdy basket or box they can reuse is a bonus. Ribbon in their favourite colour, a printed message or their name on a tag all add that last personal layer.

Keep practical things in mind too. If the basket is travelling, pack heavier items at the bottom. If it includes anything that can melt or spill, plan around that. And if it is going to an office or event, make sure it is easy to carry.
