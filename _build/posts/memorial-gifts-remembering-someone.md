Choosing something to remember a loved one is not easy. A memorial keepsake is a quiet way to keep a person close, or to give comfort to family and friends. Here are a few gentle ideas and some things to think about.

## Start with what feels right

There is no single right answer. Some families want a keepsake for the home. Others want small matching pieces for relatives, or something to place at a service. Take your time and choose what feels true to the person you are remembering.

## Keepsake ideas

- A photo crystal with a favourite picture
- An engraved crystal or wood piece with a name and dates
- A small plaque for a memorial table or garden
- A personalized keychain or small piece for each family member
- A framed or engraved piece with a short line that sounds like them

## Choosing a photo

A clear, well-lit photo works best. Faces that are sharp and close to the camera are easiest to engrave. Send the original file when you can, rather than a screenshot. If you are unsure about a photo, send it and we will tell you how it is likely to look.

> **Take your time** There is no rush to decide. Share what you have in mind and we can talk through options.

## Wording

Keep it simple. A name, the dates and one short line are plenty. Some families choose a favourite saying, a nickname or a few words that capture the person. If you are not sure what to write, we can suggest a few simple options.

## Giving to others

A small keepsake can also be a meaningful gift for family and close friends. Matching pieces with the same design can feel like a shared thank-you and a shared memory.

## Plan around the date

If there is a service or an anniversary you have in mind, share the date with your first message. Custom pieces are made to order, and each design is approved before anything is engraved. The earlier you reach out, the more choices you will have.
