A baby shower is a small, happy event, and the favour is the last thing guests touch before they leave. The best ones are useful, a little bit sweet, and tied to the theme. Here is how to plan them without stress.

## Start with the theme and the colours

Pick two or three colours and one simple idea, such as a name, a moon and stars, or a small animal. Use the same look on the invitations, the décor and the favours. When everything matches, the table looks put together and the photos look better.

## Choose something guests will use

Edible favours disappear by the end of the day. Guests are more likely to keep something they can use at home or at work.

- Personalized tumblers or water bottles with the baby's name or the date
- Small engraved wood pieces or coasters
- Keychains with a name, a date or a short message
- Tote bags printed with the theme, which guests can reuse for shopping
- Small candle or treat sets with a printed tag

## Count your guests carefully

Start with the guest list and count each person who will take a favour home. Add a few extra for late additions and for the host family. It is much easier to have a few spares than to reorder in a hurry.

> **Quick tip** Couples and families often share one favour. If you are not sure, ask the host how many households are coming, then add a small buffer.

## Keep the wording short

A name, a date or one short line is plenty. Short text engraves and prints more clearly, and it reads well from across the table. If you want a longer message, put it on a printed tag instead of the item itself.

## Think about the gift table too

If the shower has a gift table or a welcome sign, use the same design there. A matching set for the parents-to-be, such as a tote or a tumbler with the baby's name, makes a lovely keepsake as well.

## Order early and share the date

Custom favours are made to order, and the design needs to be approved before anything is engraved or printed. Send us the shower date with your first message and we will plan around it. The earlier you start, the more options you will have.

## What to send us

To get a quick quote, it helps to share:

- The date of the shower and where it is
- How many favours you need
- The theme and colours
- The name or wording you would like on each piece
