Matching apparel makes a team look unified, and it is a simple way to carry your brand to events, jobsites and the office. A little planning up front keeps the order easy. Here is a checklist.

## Decide what you are ordering

Start with the pieces your team will actually wear. T-shirts, hoodies, jackets and caps all work, and many teams mix a couple of items. Think about the season, the work environment and how often people will wear them.

## Get your logo ready

A clear logo file makes the whole process faster. If you have a vector file or a high resolution version, send that. If all you have is a small image, send it anyway and we will tell you what can be done.

- Simple logos with few colours print and stitch most cleanly
- Fine detail and gradients may need to be simplified
- Tell us the colours you want on each shirt colour

## Choose colours with contrast

The logo needs to stand out against the shirt. Light logos work well on dark shirts, and dark logos on light ones. If your brand has two colours, pick a shirt that makes both of them readable.

> **Placement matters** A small logo on the chest and a larger one on the back is a classic, and easy to read in photos.

## Collect sizes before you order

Ask everyone for their size, and ask them to check the size chart rather than guess. Include a few extras in common sizes for new hires and last-minute changes. A simple list with name, size and item keeps everything organised.

## Think about repeat orders

If you will add new staff later, keep the same design and colours so future orders match. Ask us to keep your design on file so reordering is easy.

## Plan around your date

Custom apparel is made to order, and the design needs approval before anything is printed. If you need the pieces for a launch, a trade show or a seasonal start, share the date with your first message. The earlier you reach out, the more options you will have.

## What to send us

To get a quick quote, it helps to share:

- Your logo file and any brand colours
- The items you want and roughly how many
- A list of sizes if you have it
- The date you need everything by
