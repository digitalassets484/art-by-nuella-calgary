Crystals

How a 3D photo crystal is made

Art by NuellaCalgary · October 8, 2026 · 2 min read

A photo crystal looks like the picture is floating inside solid glass. It is a popular gift for weddings, memorials, new babies, pets and milestone anniversaries. Here is how it works, in plain terms.

What a photo crystal actually is

It is a block of clear optical crystal with an image engraved inside it, not on the surface. A laser focuses on tiny points deep in the glass. Each point leaves a small white dot, and thousands of dots together form the picture.

From photo to crystal

  1. You send the photo or design you want.
  2. The image is cleaned up and converted into a pattern of points.
  3. The laser engraves the pattern inside the crystal.
  4. The finished piece is cleaned, and is often set on a light base so the image glows.

Choosing a photo that engraves well

  • Pick a sharp, well-lit photo. Blurry photos stay blurry in glass.
  • Faces that are clear and fairly close to the camera work best.
  • Simple, uncluttered backgrounds help the subject stand out.
  • Send the original file when you can, not a screenshot or a photo of a photo.
Tip If you are not sure about a photo, send it anyway. It is easier to check one photo than to guess.

2D or 3D

A 2D crystal shows a flat picture inside the glass. A 3D crystal builds a small sculpture of the subject, such as a pair of faces or a pet, so it looks like it has depth when you turn it. Which one is right depends on the photo and the shape of crystal you choose.

Good for gifts that last

Crystal does not fade or wear the way paper does, which is why it suits memorials, weddings and milestones. Engraving a name and date on the base finishes it off.
