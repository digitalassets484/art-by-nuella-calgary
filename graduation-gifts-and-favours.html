Graduation gifts and favours: what to give and when to order

Art by NuellaCalgary · November 2, 2026 · 2 min read

Graduation is a big moment, for the graduate and for everyone who helped them get there. A personal gift or party favour marks the day, and the right one stays on a desk or shelf long after the ceremony. Here is how to plan it.

Gifts for the graduate

The best graduation gifts say something about the person and the milestone. A name, a year or a short message turns an everyday item into a keepsake.

  • An engraved tumbler or water bottle for the next chapter
  • A leather notebook or journal with initials or the graduation year
  • An engraved wood piece or plaque with the program name
  • A photo crystal with a favourite graduation picture
  • A tote bag or tee printed with the class year

Favours for the party

If you are hosting a celebration, a small favour for each guest is a nice thank-you for coming. Keep it simple and useful, such as a keychain, a coaster or a small bottle with the graduate's name and the year. Choose one design and use it across all of them so the table looks coordinated.

Keep it short A name and a year read clearly on almost any material. Save longer messages for a card.

Match the colours

Many schools and programs have colours of their own. Using the same two or three colours across gifts, favours and décor makes the day feel put together, and it photographs well.

Count and add a few extra

List the graduate gifts first, then count every guest who will take a favour home. Add a small buffer for people who show up unexpectedly. It is easier than reordering in a hurry.

Plan around the date

Graduation dates do not move, so work backwards from the day. Custom pieces are made to order, and each design needs to be approved before anything is engraved or printed. Share the date with your first message and we will plan around it. The earlier you ask, the more choices you will have.

What to send us

To get a quick quote, it helps to share:

  • The graduation or party date
  • How many gifts and favours you need
  • Colours, school or program name, and the year
  • The names or wording you would like on each piece
