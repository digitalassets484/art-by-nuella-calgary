Matching tees for the whole family: a quick guide

Art by NuellaCalgary · October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Matching tees turn a family reunion, trip or celebration into a group photo everyone remembers. A little planning up front keeps it easy.

Choose the shirt colour first

Pick a shirt colour that suits everyone and the setting. Neutral colours such as black, white, navy and heather grey are safe for mixed groups. Brighter colours stand out in outdoor photos. The design colour then needs to contrast with the shirt so it reads clearly.

Keep the design bold and simple

A short line, a family name, a year or a simple graphic prints better and reads from a distance. Too many small details get lost on fabric. If you want something special, a small design on the chest with a larger one on the back is a classic.

Plan your sizes carefully

Collect sizes from everyone before you order, and include kids and babies. Ask people to check the size chart rather than guess, because fit varies between styles. It also helps to order a few extras in common sizes.

  • Make a simple list: name, size and style (adult, youth, toddler)
  • Decide whether everyone wears the same style or you allow options
  • Add a few spares for last-minute additions

Think about the whole look

Matching tees pair well with other pieces: tote bags, bottles or hats in the same design. If you are planning a larger event, a matching set makes everyone feel like part of the group.

Ask for a preview Tell us your idea and we will show you how the design will sit on the shirt before anything is printed.

Give yourself time

Printing is done to order, so the earlier you send your design and sizes, the more room there is for changes. Share your event date first so we can plan around it.
