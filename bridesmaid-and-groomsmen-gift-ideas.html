Weddings

Bridesmaid and groomsmen gift ideas that are not another mug

Art by NuellaCalgary · October 30, 2026 · 2 min read

Your wedding party spends months helping you plan, celebrate and show up on the day. A thank-you gift is a small way to say it, and the ones people keep are personal, useful and look good in photos.

Think beyond the standard mug

A plain mug is fine, but a gift with a name, a monogram or the wedding date feels like it was chosen for that person. Even a simple item becomes a keepsake when it carries a small personal detail.

Ideas for bridesmaids

  • A personalized tumbler or water bottle for the getting-ready morning
  • A monogrammed robe to wear in the photos
  • A tote bag printed with a name or short message
  • A small gift box with a few favourite things inside
  • An engraved keychain or notebook

Ideas for groomsmen

  • Engraved tumblers or bottles with names and the date
  • A leather notebook or journal with initials
  • An engraved wood piece or coaster set
  • Matching tees or hoodies for the day-before get-together
  • A small gift set with a short thank-you note

Match the set, personalize each piece

A matching set looks great in group photos. Choose the same item and design for everyone, then add each person's name or initials. That keeps the group looking coordinated while every gift still feels personal.

Good to know Using the same colours and fonts as your invitations and signs ties the whole day together.

Keep the message short

Names, initials and the wedding date work on almost any material. If you want a longer thank-you, write it on a card and tuck it inside the gift.

Count everyone

List the full wedding party, including readers, ushers and anyone else you want to thank. Add one or two spares for last-minute changes, so you are never short on the day.

Start early

Custom gifts are made to order, and each design needs to be approved first. Share your wedding date and the number of people when you first reach out. The earlier you start, the more choices you will have for items, colours and wording.
