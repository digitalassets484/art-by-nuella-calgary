Corporate gifts people actually keep

Art by NuellaCalgary · October 12, 2026 · 2 min read

Most corporate gifts end up in a drawer. The ones that get used are practical, look good and carry a logo that is quiet enough to want on a desk. Here is how to choose well.

Pick something people use every day

Drinkware, notebooks and bags get picked up daily, so your name stays in view. A well-made tumbler or bottle with a clean one-colour logo beats a pile of small items that nobody asked for.

Good options by purpose

  • Client thank-yous: engraved tumblers, wood boards or a small curated basket
  • New hire welcome kits: a bottle, a notebook and a tee or hoodie in a matching design
  • Team events and retreats: matching apparel and tumblers for everyone
  • Recognition and awards: engraved wood or crystal plaques with names and dates
  • Holiday gifts: a simple basket or boxed set with a personal note

Keep the logo simple

Logos with few colours and clean lines engrave and print best. If your logo has fine detail or gradients, send the vector or high resolution file and we will tell you what will work on each material.

Repeat orders If you will reorder for new hires or next year's event, ask us to keep your design file so the next batch matches the first.

Order ahead

Custom orders take time for design approval, making and finishing. Around the holidays, trade shows and graduation season, ask early. Tell us the date your gifts need to be in hand and we will plan back from it.

Name the details up front

When you reach out, it helps to include the item you have in mind, the quantity, the logo file, any names to add and your deadline. With that, we can quote quickly and avoid back-and-forth.

Gifting a team or your clients?

Send your logo, the item and roughly how many. We will get back to you with a quote.
