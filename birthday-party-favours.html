Birthday party favours kids (and parents) love

Art by NuellaCalgary · October 15, 2026 · 2 min read

The best party favours are the ones that go home and get used. For birthdays, that means something personal, durable and a little bit fun. Here is how to choose.

Think about who is coming

Favours for a kids' party are different from favours for a milestone birthday. Little guests like something to hold and use, such as a bottle with their name or a small bag. Adults tend to prefer a tumbler, a keychain or a coaster they will actually keep.

Ideas that work

  • Personalized water bottles or tumblers with each guest's name
  • Tote bags or drawstring bags printed with the birthday theme
  • Keychains with the name and age
  • Matching tees for the birthday crew
  • Small engraved keepsakes for milestone birthdays like 40, 50 or 70

Match the theme

Pick two or three colours and one simple graphic, then use them across the favours, the party décor and any apparel. It makes the day look put together, and it photographs well.

Count and buffer

Count every child and adult who will take a favour home, then add a few extra for surprise guests and siblings. It is easier than reordering later.

Names add up Personalizing each piece with a name takes a little more time to prepare, so tell us early if you want names on everything.

Order early

Custom favours are made to order. Give us your date, theme and guest count as soon as you have them, and we will let you know what is possible in your timeline.

Planning a birthday?

Tell us the date, the theme and how many guests. We will suggest favours that fit.
